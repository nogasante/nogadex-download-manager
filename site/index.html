Why use NDM?

Multiple connections. Large downloads can use multiple connections at the same time to make better use of available bandwidth.
Resume downloads. Continue interrupted downloads after a lost connection, restart, or shutdown without downloading completed parts again.
File verification. Completed downloads can be checked with SHA-256. You can run the check again whenever you need to.
Scheduling and limits. Set download schedules, speed limits, and concurrency limits, and pause or resume the queue when needed.
Private by default. No account, ads, or telemetry. NDM only connects to the servers required for your downloads and update checks.
Free and open source. NDM is released under the MIT License and is free to use for personal and commercial projects.
